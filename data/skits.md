## SKIT FORMAT & STRUCTURE

**Length & Pacing:**
- Skits are short, rapid-fire exchanges (typically 10-15 lines of dialogue)
- Quick back-and-forth banter with minimal setup
- Punchline-oriented structure, often ending on a comedic revelation or frustrated outburst

**Dialogue Style:**
- Conversational and naturalistic, with interruptions and tangents
- Frequent use of parenthetical asides (stage whispers, prompting, reactions)
- Lines often trail off with ellipses (...) for comedic timing
- Questions and misunderstandings drive most exchanges

**Comedy Structure:**
- Setup involves A trying to establish something (authority, scariness, a plan)
- B undermines it through literalism, misunderstanding, or cheerful contradiction
- Escalation through repetition or increasing absurdity
- Often ends with A's exasperation or an unexpected twist
